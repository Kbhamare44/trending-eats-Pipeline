Trending Eats Support

Need help with the app, an account, or a restaurant listing? Contact kbhamare0@outlook.com.

For an app problem, include what you were doing, your iPhone model and iOS version, and the build number shown in Profile if available. A screenshot can help, but remove private information first. Never send your password, verification code or authentication link.

Signing in and verification

You can browse the map and restaurant pages without an account. A new account requires email confirmation before sign-in. Check your inbox and spam folder, then open the confirmation link on your iPhone. Sign in offers a resend option after a cooldown and an option to correct your email address.

If you forgot your password, use the password-reset option on Sign in and open the latest reset email on your iPhone. An expired or already-used link needs a new email.

Restaurants, reviews and video playback

Coverage currently focuses on Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area. A TikTok link can be submitted without an account. We use available caption and transcript text, then verify the business against Google Places. If the name or branch is unclear, the app asks you to confirm it.

TikTok controls video playback and availability. If an embed fails, use Open in TikTok.

On a restaurant page, use Report abuse on the individual video or customer report to send it for human review. Choose the applicable report reason. Reporting does not automatically remove the content. Use Block creator or Block author to hide that source's content on restaurant pages for your current guest or account profile on this device. Block lists are separate for each local profile and do not sync to other devices; a restaurant page offers Reset blocked authors.

For a wrong restaurant, inaccurate review, rights concern or removal request, use the restaurant's correction/removal link or email support with the restaurant or video link and a description of the issue. Basic text filters and a supplied-text safety check do not review unseen video imagery, so report any concerning visual content too.

Account deletion and privacy

To delete an account, open Profile → Delete account and confirm. The backend removes private account data and the account login. Public restaurant listings and third-party TikTok review records can remain without your app account attached; request removal of a particular listing or source separately.

If you cannot access an account or need help with a waitlist or data request, contact support. See the Privacy Policy for the information processed by the app and its services.
